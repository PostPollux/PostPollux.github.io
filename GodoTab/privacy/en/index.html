Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

1. Controller

Godotab
Available at: https://godotab.github.io

2. Principle

We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. In our app Godotab, no personal data is collected, stored, or processed by default.

3. Data Collected

No usage data, analytics data, device data, or other personal data is collected or transmitted to us by the app itself.

4. Optional Cloud Integration (Google Drive & Dropbox)

The app offers the optional ability to connect to your Google Drive or Dropbox account.

  • Login happens directly with Google or Dropbox (OAuth).
  • Your credentials (e.g. tokens) are not transmitted to us and are not stored by us.
  • The credentials are only transferred to Google or Dropbox to enable the integration.
  • You can disconnect the integration at any time in the app.

5. Data Sharing

Personal data is only shared with third parties in the context of the cloud integration you requested:

  • Google LLC (Google Drive)
  • Dropbox, Inc.

No other sharing or selling of your data takes place.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection. Since we do not store any personal data from you, these rights are usually exercised directly with Google or Dropbox.

7. Questions

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to open an issue on GitHub.
